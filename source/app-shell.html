ADULT KIDNEY FUNCTION

Kidney function calculator

eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021 or legacy MDRD) or Cockcroft–Gault creatinine clearance. Use a quantified, IDMS-standardized creatinine within the laboratory’s reportable range.

Measure

Most drug tables here are banded by CrCl. Use the measure the drug’s table states.

Switching units converts the value already entered.

MDRD is a legacy equation. This version omits its original race multiplier; prefer CKD-EPI 2021 for a current race-free estimate.

Body size for non-indexed eGFR

Mosteller BSA. Body-size extremes and oedema require clinical judgement.

For drug dosing

Match the drug’s stated kidney-function measure and units. eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) is not Cockcroft–Gault creatinine clearance (mL/min), and the weight used for CrCl changes the result: use the weight the label or local protocol specifies. No dose or dialysis prescription is selected or changed by this calculator.

Near a dosing threshold, consider a combined creatinine–cystatin C estimate or measured assessment. Atypical muscle mass, frailty or critical illness may make creatinine estimates unreliable.

Equations, limits & sources

For adults aged 18–120 in this app. No result is provided for AKI/changing creatinine, dialysis or pregnancy. A result does not establish CKD chronicity. For ages 18–25, consider comparison with CKiD U25.

CKD-EPI 2021: 142 × min(SCr/κ,1)α × max(SCr/κ,1)−1.200 × 0.9938age × 1.012 if female. κ = 0.7 female / 0.9 male; α = −0.241 female / −0.302 male.

MDRD, IDMS: 175 × SCr−1.154 × age−0.203 × 0.742 if female. The original race multiplier is omitted. This is a legacy comparison, not a newly refitted race-free MDRD equation. Interpret higher-GFR MDRD estimates cautiously.

Cockcroft–Gault: CrCl (mL/min) = (140 − age) × weight (kg) ÷ (72 × SCr) × 0.85 if female. Ideal body weight (Devine) = 50 kg male / 45.5 kg female + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft. Adjusted body weight = IBW + 0.4 × (actual − IBW), shown only when actual weight exceeds IBW. Creatinine is not rounded up. Results are not indexed to body surface area. The equation was derived before creatinine assays were standardized, so with today’s IDMS-traceable creatinine it may give somewhat higher values than in the original drug studies.

SCr is in mg/dL; µmol/L is divided by 88.4. Indexed results are in mL/min/1.73 m². Mosteller BSA = √(height in cm × weight in kg / 3600). Non-indexed eGFR = indexed eGFR × BSA / 1.73; it remains an estimate of GFR, not CrCl.

CONTRAST · ADULTS

Contrast-associated AKI risk

Risk group for iodinated contrast. IV contrast (CT) uses the ACR–NKF consensus eGFR thresholds; intra-arterial coronary angiography or PCI uses the Mehran score. Gadolinium (MRI) risk is nephrogenic systemic fibrosis, not AKI: see each gadolinium agent’s entry and ACR group.

Route
IV contrast
Mehran score
How to use

A risk group supports, but does not replace, the decision between the clinical need for a contrast study and its kidney risk. Contrast-associated AKI (any AKI within 48 h) is not the same as AKI caused by contrast; much AKI after IV contrast is coincident. No contrast dose, hydration order or dialysis prescription is selected by this tool.

Values stay in this tab and clear when this panel closes. No identifiers, results or entries are stored or transmitted.

Method, limits & sources

IV contrast (ACR–NKF 2020): high risk = AKI or eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m², including dialysis patients who still pass urine. IV isotonic saline prophylaxis at eGFR <30 or AKI (not on dialysis) unless contraindicated; optional at 30–44 in individual high-risk circumstances; not indicated at stable eGFR ≥45. These statements apply to IV, not intra-arterial, contrast.

Mehran score (2004): hypotension 5, IABP 5, heart failure 5, age >75 years 4, anaemia 3, diabetes 3, contrast 1 point per complete 100 mL, eGFR 40–<60 = 2, 20–<40 = 4, <20 = 6. Bands: ≤5 low (CIN 7.5%, dialysis 0.04%); 6–10 moderate (14.0%, 0.12%); 11–15 high (26.1%, 1.09%); ≥16 very high (57.3%, 12.6%). CIN was defined as a rise in serum creatinine ≥25% or ≥0.5 mg/dL at 48 h after PCI.

The score was derived in a PCI population using MDRD eGFR; the app’s eGFR is CKD-EPI 2021, so results near a band edge are approximate. The original paper also offers a serum-creatinine version (>1.5 mg/dL = 4); this tool uses the eGFR version. Not validated for IV contrast or for gadolinium.
